1. Introduction

Wondersale ("we", "our", or "us") operates the retail point-of-sale and store operating system located at os.wonder.sale. We respect your personal privacy and are committed to protecting it in compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, and applicable consumer laws of India.

This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, store, handle, and protect customer personal information collected in connection with store purchases, digital tax invoices, and customer loyalty memberships.

2. Information We Collect

When you shop at Wondersale retail outlets or receive digital invoices, we may collect:

  • Contact Information: Customer phone number and optionally customer name, provided voluntarily at the checkout counter.
  • Transaction Data: Invoice number, store branch, purchase date/time, purchased items, tax breakdowns (CGST/SGST), and payment method (Cash, UPI, Card, Store Credit).
  • Customer Loyalty/VIP Program Data: Loyalty points, VIP membership tier, accumulated balance, and purchase frequency.

🔒 We do not store complete debit/credit card numbers or UPI MPINs. All electronic payments are processed directly through certified RBI-compliant banking networks and payment terminals.

3. Purpose of Processing & Use of WhatsApp Messaging

We process your contact information for the following specific purposes:

  • Digital Invoices & Receipts: Delivering legal GST invoices and return/exchange vouchers via WhatsApp or SMS to your phone to support paperless, green retail operations.
  • Customer Support & Returns: Locating your bill upon presentation of your phone number or digital invoice when requesting a product exchange, repair, or warranty claim.
  • Transaction Verification: Preventing fraud, verifying VIP card points redemptions, and reconciling cash register settlements.

4. WhatsApp Business Messaging Compliance

Wondersale utilizes the official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API. We strictly adhere to Meta's WhatsApp Commerce Policy and WhatsApp Business Terms of Service:

  • We send transactional utility messages (tax receipts and return vouchers) only to customers who purchase from our store.
  • We do not send unsolicited commercial spam or bulk cold marketing messages without express consent.
  • Customers may opt out of receiving digital receipts via WhatsApp at any time by requesting printed paper receipts at the counter.

5. Data Retention & Security

We employ industry-standard encryption, SSL/TLS transmission protocols, access controls, and firewall protections to safeguard all transaction and customer records. Billing and taxation data is retained solely for the statutory periods mandated by the Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act, 2017 and Indian retail regulations.

6. Sharing of Personal Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third-party data brokers. Data is shared only with:

  • Authorized communication providers (Meta Platforms Inc.) strictly for delivering requested WhatsApp digital invoices.
  • Governmental or judicial authorities when strictly required by applicable Indian tax laws or legal process.

7. Customer Rights & Grievance Officer

Under Indian data protection laws, you have the right to review, update, or request the deletion of your customer loyalty profile.

For inquiries, grievances, or privacy-related requests, please contact our designated Grievance Officer:

  • Entity: Wondersale Retail Store Operations
  • Email: support@wonder.sale
